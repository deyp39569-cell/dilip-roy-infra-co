Sequence

From survey peg to handover.

A fixed four-stage sequence keeps every site — regardless of scale — accountable to the same schedule and the same site register.

01

Survey & Design

Topographic survey, soil testing, and structural design validated against site conditions.

02

Approvals & Mobilisation

Statutory clearances secured, site mobilised, and material sourcing locked to specification.

03

Construction & Quality

Phased execution with third-party material testing at every pour and every layer.

04

Handover & Maintenance

As-built documentation delivered, with defect-liability support through the maintenance period.

The Founder

Site-first, from the first contract to the last.

Dilip Roy founded Roy Infrastructure on a simple working rule: every commitment made in a tender is a commitment made on-site, and the two should never drift apart. That principle has carried the firm from single-culvert contracts to multi-kilometre highway and water infrastructure packages.

He continues to be directly involved in project planning and quality sign-off across all active sites, working alongside site engineers rather than at a distance from them.

"An infrastructure company is judged years after handover — not on the day of it. We build for that inspection."
— Dilip Roy
